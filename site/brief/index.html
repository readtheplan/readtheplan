The loop

Public signal, delivered as a free brief.

  1. Monitor public infra, security, compliance, and readtheplan project sources.
  2. Filter for changes a platform team can act on this week.
  3. Analyze the Terraform/SOC 2 risk angle without collecting raw customer plans.
  4. Package the top items into a concise operator-ready issue.
  5. Publish the reviewed artifact for the whole infrastructure community.

Readers

Who the artifact serves.

  • Platform and SRE teams that own Terraform review gates.
  • DevOps consultancies managing client infrastructure portfolios.
  • SOC 2 consultants who need cloud-control change context.
  • Seed-stage infra and devtool startups tracking buyer pain.

Deliverable structure

Each issue has the same useful shape.

Section Purpose Output
Top 5 infra/compliance changes Keep attention on the highest-signal items. Short ranked list with source category and affected control theme.
Why they matter Translate release notes and security notes into operator impact. Plain-language risk, blast radius, and review priority.
Terraform/SOC2 risk angle Connect cloud changes to plan review and audit evidence. IAM, logging, networking, data, change management, and evidence mapping.
Action checklist Make the brief operational. Concrete checks for CI, reviewers, control owners, and consultancies.
readtheplan CTA Turn useful context into open-source adoption or contribution. Local CLI, playground, MCP tools, docs, or a community contribution.

Sustainability

Keep access free; monetize around the edges.

  • Every issue and every shipped readtheplan feature stays free.
  • Future sponsorships must be clearly labeled and never influence risk guidance.
  • Machine traffic may be monetized through Cloudflare when beta access is available.
  • No behavioral ads, paid rankings, private edition, or feature-gated upsell.
