Weekly change intelligence

Weekly Terraform/SOC 2 change intelligence for platform teams.

A free community brief for teams that need infra and compliance signal every week: monitor, filter, analyze, package, publish. It is a decision-ready artifact, not an autonomous agent.

Local-first posture No raw Terraform plan upload No backend No accounts or billing

The first slice is static: a landing page, a sample issue, and a runbook. It does not activate monitoring, scheduled jobs, hosted analysis, storage, forms, or delivery automation.

Suggest a topic on GitHub

Public suggestions and contributions keep the brief useful and free.

The loop

Repeated open-source signal, delivered as a brief.

  1. Monitor public infra, security, compliance, and readtheplan project sources.
  2. Filter for changes a platform team can act on this week.
  3. Analyze the Terraform/SOC 2 risk angle without collecting raw customer plans.
  4. Package the top items into a concise operator-ready issue.
  5. Publish the reviewed artifact for the community.

Readers

Who the artifact helps.

  • Platform and SRE teams that own Terraform review gates.
  • DevOps consultancies managing client infrastructure portfolios.
  • SOC 2 consultants who need cloud-control change context.
  • Seed-stage infra and devtool startups tracking operator pain.

Deliverable structure

Each issue has the same useful shape.

Section Purpose Output
Top 5 infra/compliance changes Keep attention on the highest-signal items. Short ranked list with source category and affected control theme.
Why they matter Translate release notes and security notes into operator impact. Plain-language risk, blast radius, and review priority.
Terraform/SOC2 risk angle Connect cloud changes to plan review and audit evidence. IAM, logging, networking, data, change management, and evidence mapping.
Action checklist Make the brief operational. Concrete checks for CI, reviewers, control owners, and consultancies.
readtheplan CTA Turn the brief into a practical, free next step. Local CLI, setup generator, MCP preview, or custom integration next step.

Community ladder

Start with signal, then share the workflow.

  • Public sample: prove the format with an evergreen issue.
  • Open weekly brief: curate useful changes for every reader.
  • Community suggestions: propose sources, keywords, and control priorities.
  • Reusable integrations: contribute local-first review workflows back to the project.
