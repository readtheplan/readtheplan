Privacy Policy

Effective: July 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Local toolchain: The CLI, GitHub Action, and MCP tools do not transmit plans or usage data to readtheplan servers. They run on your machine or CI runner.

Website: Cloudflare processes ordinary request data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP addresses, request headers, requested URLs, and security signals. We may use aggregate, cookie-less analytics to understand site traffic. We do not use behavioral advertising, tracking pixels, or cross-site profiles.

AI chat: If you use the optional website chat, the messages you submit are sent through a Cloudflare Pages Function to our configured AI provider so it can generate a response. Do not paste infrastructure plans, credentials, personal data, or other sensitive information into chat.

2. How We Use Information

We process the limited website information described above to:

  • Deliver, secure, debug, and improve the website.
  • Rate-limit abuse of the optional AI chat.
  • Generate chat responses when you explicitly submit a message.
  • Measure aggregate human and automated traffic.

3. Data Storage and Retention

readtheplan does not operate user accounts or store infrastructure plans. The website is primarily static. Chat messages are processed to answer the request; infrastructure and retention practices of Cloudflare and the configured AI provider are governed by their respective policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent visitor data. Information is shared only:

  • With Cloudflare to deliver and protect the website.
  • With the configured AI provider when you submit an AI chat message.
  • As required by law or legal process.
  • With your explicit consent.
  • To protect the security or integrity of the Service.

5. Automated Access and Monetization

We may use Cloudflare controls to allow, block, measure, or charge automated crawlers for access to public website content. These controls apply to automated requests for public content. They do not monetize CLI activity, infrastructure plans, chat messages, or personal data, and they do not place a paywall in front of human visitors.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, security headers, input limits, and rate limiting. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security, so do not send secrets or raw infrastructure plans through website chat.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Use the local toolchain without creating a readtheplan account.
  • Ask what website information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of information we control, where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact info@readtheplan.dev.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above identifies the current version.
