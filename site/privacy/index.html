Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

OSS Toolchain: The local CLI, GitHub Action, and MCP tools do not transmit any data to readtheplan servers. We collect nothing.

Website: Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. Plausible receives only the allowlisted activation events listed below; the general Plausible pageview script is not loaded. We do not use behavioral advertising scripts or third-party pixels.

Activation events: The site may send only these Plausible custom-event names: verify_change_click when the homepage playground link is activated; copy_install after the install command is copied successfully; playground_run after the first successful local playground analysis in a page view; generate_ci after generated CI text is copied successfully; and setup_help_click when an explicit setup-help email link is activated. A click or copy does not prove that a plan was verified, a command was run, a gate was installed, or an email was sent.

Activation events have no custom properties and never include raw plans, repository contents, file names, credentials, or command output. Every event uses the same fixed synthetic URL, https://readtheplan.dev/activation; the visited page URL, query or hash values, referrer, plan sizes, resource addresses, finding counts, risk results, framework choices, and repository identifiers are not sent.

AI chat: Chat messages are sent to DeepSeek, a third-party processor, to produce a reply. Do not submit secrets, raw infrastructure plans, personal data, or other sensitive material. The chat is not required to use readtheplan.

Email: If you choose to request free setup help, your email provider transmits the message you compose. The static site does not submit an intake form or attach a plan.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, secure, and improve the public documentation and demos.
  • Answer optional project-guide chat messages.
  • Respond when you voluntarily request free setup help.

3. Data Storage and Retention

readtheplan.dev does not offer product accounts, billing, or a managed plan-analysis backend. Local plan artifacts and evidence stay in your environment. Cloudflare and DeepSeek process only the website traffic or optional chat data described above under their respective service policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties except:

  • As required by law or legal process.
  • With your explicit consent.
  • To protect the security or integrity of the Service.

5. Security

The public site uses encrypted connections, security headers, a restrictive Content Security Policy, and rate limiting on the optional chat endpoint. The site has no readtheplan account passwords or billing records to store.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Ask what information a voluntary email exchange contains.
  • Request deletion of information you sent directly to the project, subject to legal obligations.

To exercise these rights, contact info@readtheplan.dev.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.
