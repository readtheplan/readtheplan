FAQ

Do I need to upload Terraform plans?
No. readtheplan is local-first by design. Raw infrastructure plans stay on your machine or CI runner unless you explicitly send them somewhere else.
Are team and compliance features free too?
Yes. The CLI, CI integrations, MCP tools, signed evidence, custom rules, and all built-in compliance catalogs are part of the open-source project.
Will a paid tier appear later?
There is no paid product tier planned. Future hosted conveniences, if any, must remain optional and cannot remove capabilities from the local toolchain.
Does website monetization affect my privacy?
It should not. We do not use behavioral advertising or sell visitor data. Any monetization applies only to selected commercial automation accessing public content or future hosted compute. It never applies to human access, CLI usage, local plan analysis, or personal data.
Which compliance frameworks are supported?
Six built-in catalogs cover SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP Moderate, and HITRUST. Custom rules are also supported.
