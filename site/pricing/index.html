FAQ

Do I need to upload Terraform plans?
No. readtheplan is local-first by design. Raw Terraform plan JSON never leaves your machine or CI runner. The optional AI chat is separate and clearly discloses its third-party processor.
Is every feature really free?
Yes. The CLI, GitHub Action, local MCP tools, evidence formats, built-in rules, and compliance catalogs are MIT licensed. There is no feature-gated edition.
How is the project sustained?
Community contributions, project sponsorship when available, and optional machine-scale monetization experiments that do not restrict normal human use.
Can my company contribute an adapter or rule pack?
Yes. The adapter and rule registries are extensible, and contributions are reviewed under the same public test and security gates as built-in support.
Which compliance frameworks are supported?
Six built-in catalogs cover SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP Moderate, and HITRUST. Custom mappings can be scoped separately.
