FAQ

Do I need to upload Terraform plans?
No. readtheplan is local-first by design. Raw Terraform plan JSON never leaves your machine or CI runner. Enterprise support does not change that boundary unless a separate, explicit architecture and data-handling agreement is established.
Can I start with OSS and add support later?
Yes. The OSS CLI, GitHub Action, evidence formats, and compliance catalogs remain the foundation. Enterprise work builds on those same public tools.
What does Enterprise include?
Scope is tailored to your environment and may include private deployment guidance, custom rules or framework mappings, onboarding, and negotiated support.
Can we run a pilot first?
Yes. Email info@readtheplan.dev with your Terraform workflow and compliance goals so we can define a small, explicit pilot scope.
Which compliance frameworks are supported?
Six built-in catalogs cover SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP Moderate, and HITRUST. Custom mappings can be scoped separately.
