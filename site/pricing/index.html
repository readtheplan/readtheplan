FAQ

Do I need to upload Terraform plans?
No. readtheplan is local-first by design. Raw Terraform plan JSON never leaves your machine. The managed platform only stores metadata: org/project info, policy profiles, and signed analysis summaries.
Can I switch from OSS to Managed later?
Yes. The OSS CLI and Managed platform share the same rules engine and compliance catalog. Migration is a one-time setup: connect your CLI, configure your org policies, and you're running.
What's the difference between Managed and Enterprise?
Enterprise adds a private connector that runs entirely within your infrastructure — no data leaves your network. It also includes SSO, custom compliance frameworks, and dedicated support with a negotiated SLA.
Is there a free trial for Managed?
We offer a 14-day pilot with onboarding support. Email info@readtheplan.dev to get started.
Which compliance frameworks are supported?
SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA are built-in. Enterprise customers can request custom framework mappings (FedRAMP, PCI DSS, GDPR, etc.).
