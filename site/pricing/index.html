FAQ

Do I need to upload Terraform plans?
No. readtheplan is local-first by design. Raw Terraform plan JSON stays on your machine or CI runner. The optional website chat is separate and clearly discloses that its messages are processed by DeepSeek.
Are team and compliance features free too?
Yes. Evidence signing, agent gates, the GitHub Action, MCP server, custom rules, and all six built-in compliance catalogs are part of the free project.
How is the project funded?
The project does not charge users or sell feature access. Sustainability may come from clearly labeled sponsorships, donations, or future additive machine-facing services. Human access, normal search, releases, and local tooling remain free.
Are there ads or behavioral tracking?
No. The site does not use behavioral advertising scripts or lock content behind an ad wall. Cloudflare and cookie-less analytics protect and measure the public site without changing the free product promise.
Which compliance frameworks are supported?
Six built-in catalogs cover SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP Moderate, and HITRUST. You can also author custom rules locally.
