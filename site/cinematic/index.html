readtheplan

Terraform plans are a wall of JSON and uncertainty. We turn them into stable risk tiers, compliance evidence, and signed attestations.

$ readtheplan analyze plan.json

Four risk tiers. Zero ambiguity.

Every resource change classified instantly — safe to apply, needs review, dangerous, or irreversible.

✓

Safe

Non-destructive changes that won't break anything

⚠

Review

Modifications that need human validation before apply

!

Danger

Destructive operations that will cause downtime or data loss

☠

Irreversible

Permanent changes — deletion of state-critical resources

Compliance evidence, auto-mapped

Every resource tagged with the control IDs it audits — SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA. Generate evidence packs from the CLI without spreadsheet gymnastics.

Three steps. That's it.

1. Export your plan: terraform plan -json > plan.json
2. Run classification: readtheplan analyze plan.json
3. Review risk tiers, compliance mapping, and sign attestations
